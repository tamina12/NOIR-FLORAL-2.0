# NOIR FLORAL 🌹

### Flowers, composed like art.

A luxury digital experience concept for a fictional floral atelier based in **Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan**.

**NOIR FLORAL** combines editorial design, botanical aesthetics, creative coding and immersive interactions to create a premium florist website concept.

> Concept project by **Tamin Group**

---

## ✦ Concept

NOIR FLORAL is imagined as a modern floral atelier where flowers are treated not simply as products, but as **objects of art, emotion and composition**.

The website was designed to feel closer to a luxury fashion editorial than a traditional flower shop.

### Visual direction

* Dark editorial aesthetic
* Ivory and deep burgundy palette
* Elegant serif typography
* Large botanical photography
* Asymmetrical layouts
* Minimal technical typography
* Creative-code inspired interactions
* Premium product presentation
* Responsive experience
